## About Me

I am a data scientist with a foundation in statistics and a strong interest in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and biomedical research.

My current work focuses on building models and analytical workflows that are not only accurate, but also reliable, interpretable, statistically sound, and reproducible.

My main interests include:

- Healthcare and biomedical AI
- Medical imaging and radiomics
- Explainable and trustworthy machine learning
- Uncertainty-aware modelling
- Biomedical knowledge graphs
- Statistical learning and model validation
- Applied data analytics and visualization

> Strong AI should not only make accurate predictions — it should also provide evidence that those predictions can be trusted.
